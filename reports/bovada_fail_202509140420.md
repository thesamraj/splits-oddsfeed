### Root Cause
Bovada collector is successfully fetching data from bovada.lv but quarantining ALL events due to:
1. **Low realness scores** (0.56-0.78) below threshold (0.85)
2. **Event diversity** too low (0.07-0.12)
3. **Price variance** issues (0.40)

### Recommended Fix
1. Lower REALNESS_THRESHOLD from 0.85 to 0.50 in docker-compose.do.yml
2. OR disable realness checks temporarily with REAL_ONLY=false
3. OR investigate why Bovada's actual data is scoring so low
